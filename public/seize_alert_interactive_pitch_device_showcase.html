Zero-Irritation Engineering

Engineered for 24/7 Hypoallergenic Comfort

Contact dermatitis and metal allergy are the leading reasons patients abandon continuous neurological wearables. SEIZE ALERT strictly adheres to medical standards.

USP Class VI Liquid Silicone

The cervical collar is molded from 100% medical-grade Liquid Silicone Rubber (LSR). It is latex-free, chemically non-porous, and resistant to sweat buildup during day and night wear.

ISO 10993 Cytotoxicity Tested

Zero-Nickel Ceramic & Titanium

Nickel is the leading allergen in consumer smart bands. All structural hinges and skin-facing case backings use Grade-5 Surgical Titanium and biocompatible Zirconia ceramic (ZrO2).
