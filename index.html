The "Passive Monitor" Trap
Existing monitors (smartwatches, bed sensors) are purely passive. They send an alert after a seizure starts, but provide zero medical help. If the caregiver is asleep or in another room, the patient suffers prolonged hypoxia, brain trauma from falls, or SUDEP.

SEIZE ALERT couples edge TinyML detection with immediate auricular transcutaneous vagus nerve stimulation (tVNS). By stimulating the cymba conchae, afferent signals rapidly activate the locus coeruleus and NTS, aborting hypersynchronous thalamocortical seizure bursts before motor culmination.
